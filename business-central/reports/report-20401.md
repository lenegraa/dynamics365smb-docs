# Quality Inspection - Certificate of Analysis (report)

The **Quality Inspection - Certificate of Analysis** report documents the outcome of a finished quality inspection, including the inspected item and tracking details, each test with its value and result, any promoted result conditions, and the inspector and approver who signed off. The report uses a Word layout by default and includes company and contact information so it can be sent directly to customers as formal evidence of analysis.

You can filter the report by source item number, variant code, lot number, serial number, package number, source document number, inspection number, re-inspection number, template code, and by test code on the inspection lines, so you can produce a certificate for a specific shipment, batch, or test scope.

Quality inspectors and QA managers can use the report to:

* Issue a Certificate of Analysis for a completed inspection before releasing a lot to a customer.
* Document each test, its measured value, and the resulting pass or fail status on a single signed document.
* Include re-inspection details when an item has been re-tested after a previous result.
* Provide approver and inspector signatures and titles for compliance records.,Sales and customer service teams can use the report to:

* Send customers a formal certificate that confirms an order meets agreed quality specifications.
* Produce certificates filtered by source document or lot number to match a specific shipment.

* Retain signed certificates that show who finished the inspection and who approved the results.
