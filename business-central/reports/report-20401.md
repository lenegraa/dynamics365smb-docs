# Quality Inspection - Certificate of Analysis (report)

The **Quality Inspection - Certificate of Analysis** report produces a formal certificate that summarizes a finished quality inspection, including the inspected item and tracking details, every test with its recorded value and result, condition labels, and the names and signatures of the inspector who finished the inspection and the approver.

You can filter the report by source item number, variant code, lot number, serial number, package number, source document number, inspection number, re-inspection number, and template code so you can produce a certificate for a specific shipment, batch, or tracked unit, and you can narrow the test lines by test code.

## Use cases

[!INCLUDE [report-20401-scenario](../includes/report-20401-scenario-include.md)]

Quality managers and inspectors can use the report to:

* Issue a signed certificate of analysis for a finished inspection before releasing a lot or shipment.
* Document each test code, recorded value, result, and condition for audit and traceability purposes.
* Confirm who finished the inspection and who approved it, including job titles and signatures.

Sales and customer service teams can use the report to:

* Send a certificate of analysis to a customer together with the delivery of a specific item, lot, or serial number.
* Answer customer questions about tested attributes and conditions for a shipped batch.
* Reproduce a re-inspection certificate when an item has been re-tested.

Regulatory and compliance officers can use the report to:

* Provide auditors with evidence that inspections were completed, signed, and approved.
* Verify that recorded results meet the conditions defined in the inspection template.
* Archive certificates per inspection number and re-inspection number for regulated products.
